No sign-in or account is required to use any part of the app. The app is played in landscape; please hold the device in landscape.

HOW TO FIND EACH FEATURE
Tap Enter on the main menu to load the museum (loading takes a few seconds). On first entry, a tutorial card explains the controls.

1. Built-in tutorial for first-time visitors: appears automatically the first time you enter the museum and stays on screen until you tap "Got it". Tap the "?" button at the top right to show it again at any time.

2. Exhibit plaques with specifications and history: glowing cyan rings on the floor mark each exhibit. Walk onto the ring in front of any aircraft with the joystick (bottom left); the exhibit panel for that aircraft opens and the view turns to face it. Tap "Information" for the plaque and history, or "Statistics" for the manufacturer, first flight, wingspan and top speed. The nearest exhibits are a few steps ahead and to the right of where you start.

3. AI museum guide with spoken answers: on the same exhibit panel, tap "AI Docent", tap the "Enter Text..." field, type a question about the aircraft (for example "What was it designed for?") and press Return on the keyboard. The answer appears in the panel and is read aloud. This feature needs an internet connection; if there is none, the panel says so.

4. Two switchable exhibit layouts: tap the "Layout" button at the top left. It switches between "Multirole Fighters" and "Stealth & Strike", and the aircraft on display change.

5. Day-night cycle: the light in the hall changes through the day, most visibly through the windows along the right-hand wall; one full day lasts three minutes. Tap "Settings" at the top right to turn the Day / Night Cycle on or off.

CHANGES IN THIS BUILD (1.0, build 4)
In the previous review these features could not be located. On a touch device they were only reachable by walking into unmarked areas, the tutorial described keyboard and mouse controls, and the Settings screen could not be opened on iPad or iPhone. This build makes every feature reachable and signposted:
- A touch tutorial replaces the keyboard-and-mouse one on iPhone and iPad, stays until dismissed, and can be reopened with "?".
- A Settings button now appears at the top right on iPhone and iPad (previously Settings could only be opened with a keyboard's Escape key).
- A Layout button at the top left switches exhibit layouts from anywhere in the museum.
- Glowing floor markers show where to stand for every exhibit, and an on-screen hint explains them until the first exhibit is opened. Stepping onto a marker turns the view to face the exhibit panel.
- The Statistics panel now also shows each aircraft's manufacturer and first flight, as the App Store description states.
- The day-night cycle now runs over three minutes instead of thirty seconds, so it reads as a gradual change of light rather than flicker.
