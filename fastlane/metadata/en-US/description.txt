Step inside a virtual aviation museum and walk among legendary military aircraft — then open them up and see what is inside.

AVIA Museum is a fully explorable 3D museum. Wander the exhibit hall in first person, get up close to nine iconic jets, and learn the story behind each aircraft from its exhibit plaque: manufacturer, first flight, wingspan, top speed, and a concise history.

CUTAWAY AND COCKPIT VIEWS
Every aircraft can be opened up in the Cockpit & Cutaway hangar. A section plane slices away the near half of the airframe to reveal the engines, fuel tanks, radar, ejection seat, cannon and weapons bays inside, each one numbered and explained — the F-22's thrust-vectoring F119s, the A-10's seven-barrel cannon and its titanium armour, the B-2's four buried engines and rotary weapons bays. Or take the pilot's seat and look around a real modelled cockpit through a head-up display.

EXHIBIT BADGES
Every aircraft has its own five-question quiz drawn from its exhibit plaque and its systems. Score four or more and you earn that aircraft's badge; collect all nine to become a Hangar Master.

AI MUSEUM GUIDE
Have a question the plaque doesn't answer? Walk up to any aircraft, type your question, and get an instant answer about that specific plane — read aloud with natural text-to-speech, just like a real museum tour guide.
