Step inside a virtual aviation museum and walk among legendary military aircraft — no ticket required.

AVIA Museum is a fully explorable 3D museum. Wander the exhibit hall in first person, get up close to nine iconic jets, and learn the story behind each aircraft from its exhibit plaque: manufacturer, first flight, wingspan, top speed, and a concise history.

Have a question the plaque doesn't answer? Ask the built-in AI museum guide. Walk up to any aircraft, type your question, and get an instant answer about that specific plane — read aloud with natural text-to-speech, just like a real museum tour guide.

• Dynamic day-night cycle around the museum

Perfect for aviation enthusiasts, students, aspiring pilots, and anyone curious about modern military aviation.

AVIA Museum is free to explore.
