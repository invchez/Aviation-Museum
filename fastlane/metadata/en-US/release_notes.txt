Two new ways to explore the collection:

• Cockpit & Cutaway hangar — open any aircraft with a section plane to see the engines, fuel, radar, seat, cannon and weapons bays inside, each numbered and explained, or sit in the pilot's seat and look around with a head-up display.
• Exhibit Badges — a five-question quiz for every aircraft, drawn from its plaque and its systems. Earn all nine badges to become a Hangar Master.

Also in this update: wingspans on every plaque now read in both feet and metres.
